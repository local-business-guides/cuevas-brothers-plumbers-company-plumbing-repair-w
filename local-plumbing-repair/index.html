What a repair can involve

The work depends on what the inspection finds. A plumber may need to locate the source of a leak, check a fixture or connection, or assess a blockage. The plumber should explain the problem and the proposed repair in plain language before you approve the work.

Key facts to keep in mind:
- What it covers: The assessment and repair depend on the specific plumbing issue and the parts or area involved.
- Typical timing: There is no single duration for every repair. Diagnosis, access to the affected area, and whether parts are needed all affect timing.
- What affects cost: The cause of the problem, the amount of work, access, and any needed parts can affect the total. Ask for the cost and scope before authorizing work.
